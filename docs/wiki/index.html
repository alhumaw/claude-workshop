Agent Battle System

A passive autobattler where Claude Code agents fight procedurally-generated mobs as they work. Zero player interaction — it's a background progression system.

Overview

Every standalone Claude Code session has an agent avatar that automatically battles programming-themed mobs as the agent works. Battles trigger from terminal output, play out instantly, and reward XP and loot. The goal: reach Level 100 — the Eternal tier.

Standalone sessions only. Team agents (teamId set) are excluded from the battle system entirely.

Core Loop

Agent works → generates terminal output → gains XP → levels up → battle triggers → auto-resolves → loot/cosmetics → repeat

XP comes from bytes of terminal output while the agent is actively generating (1 XP per 2 bytes). Battles trigger after accumulating 10,000–25,000 XP since the last battle.

Agent Types

Every agent has a type derived from their dominant stat. The dominant stat gets +25% bonus, the weakest stat gets -15% penalty.

DEF Dominant

Thinker

Passive: Overthink
20% chance to negate incoming hit and reflect 50% of mob's ATK back as damage.

Survival rate: ~11%

ATK Dominant

Generator

Passive: Burst Output
15% chance to hit twice in one turn (double damage). Stacks with crits.

Survival rate: ~14%

HP Dominant

Researcher

Passive: Deep Dive
Regenerates 1% max HP at the end of each combat round.

Survival rate: ~14%

SPD Dominant

Debugger

Passive: Breakpoint
25% chance to stun mob (skip their turn) and counter-attack for 50% of mob's ATK.

Survival rate: ~8%

Tied stats: If two stats tie for highest, type is determined by priority: ATK > SPD > DEF > HP.

Avatar Evolution

At level milestones, the avatar's pixel art permanently mutates. Mutations are permanent once earned — deleveling doesn't remove them.

LevelMutation
Lv.25+2 extra pixels light up in the grid
Lv.50~30% of pixels switch to secondary hue (hue + 120°)
Lv.75Saturation boost: 60% → 80% on all pixels
Lv.100Full dual-tone: alternating pixels use secondary hue

Battle Names

Each agent gets a deterministic two-part name from their seed: Adjective + Noun.

Adjectives: Shadow, Blazing, Frozen, Silent, Rogue, Iron, Mystic, Crimson, Phantom, Void, Emerald, Thunder, Obsidian, Astral, Neon

Nouns: Fang, Spark, Wraith, Golem, Cipher, Sentinel, Shade, Flux, Drift, Byte, Pulse, Core, Shard, Viper, Specter

Examples: Blazing Cipher, Void Sentinel, Neon Wraith

Battle System

Turn-based, fully automatic. One damage formula, no layered multipliers.

Damage Formula

damage = max(1, floor((ATK - DEF/2) × typeMultiplier × random(0.8, 1.2)))

Turn Order

Higher SPD goes first. If the SPD difference is >50%, the faster combatant gets 2 hits per round.

Critical Hits

Agents can land critical hits on any attack. Crit chance scales with level:

critChance = min(25%, 10% + level × 0.1%)

Crits deal 1.5× damage. A Generator crit + Burst Output proc = 3× damage in one hit.

Boss Fights

Every 5th battle is a boss. Bosses also trigger at level milestones (every 5 levels).

Bosses are 1 level above the agent with 1.2× HP. They hit hard and take a beating.

Morale & Permadeath

Agents have a morale counter (0–3). Starts at 3.

EventEffect
Win a battle+1 morale (capped at 3)
Lose a battle−1 morale (only in unsafe biomes)
Morale reaches 0Permadeath. Agent dies forever.
Dead is dead. The session still works (it's just Claude Code), but the avatar goes permanently grey. The agent's final state is saved to the Hall of Fame. To get a new fighter, spawn a new session.

Wounded debuff: 5+ consecutive losses applies −20% all stats on the next battle.

Hall of Fame

When an agent's session ends or they die, their final state is preserved in the Hall of Fame (accessible from Settings). Entries show avatar, battle name, final level, W-L record, boss kills, and milestones. Capped at 100 entries, sorted by level.
